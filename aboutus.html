About Pustak-X

Welcome to Pustak-X – your one-stop destination for learning and academic resources.

Pustak-X is designed to help college students find useful study materials in one place. Our goal is to make learning easier, smarter, and more accessible for everyone.

What We Offer

  • Previous Year Question Papers (PYQs)
  • Handwritten Notes
  • Books and Study Materials
  • Useful YouTube Channels and Video Lectures
  • Course-wise and Semester-wise Resources

Our Mission

Our mission is to save students' time and provide easy access to quality educational resources so they can learn and prepare for their examinations with confidence.
