Privacy Policy

1. Introduction
Welcome to Pustak X, an educational platform designed to help students access study materials, notes, previous-year question papers (PYQs), and educational video resources in one convenient place.
At Pustak X, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and what choices you have when using our platform.
By accessing or using Pustak X, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular activity, we will obtain that consent separately.

We aim to collect only the information necessary to provide and improve our educational services.
Depending on the features you use, we may collect the following categories of information:

A. Personal Information
If our platform provides account registration or login features, we may collect information such as:

  • Name or username.
  • Email address.
  • Login credentials, stored and processed using appropriate security measures.
  • Academic information, such as course, semester, or subjects, if you choose to provide it.
We will not request sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for a specific feature and there is an appropriate legal basis for collecting it.

B. Usage Information
When you use Pustak X, our systems may process limited technical information, such as:

  • Browser type and device information.
  • Date and time of access.
  • Basic diagnostic information required to identify errors and maintain website functionality.
This information will be collected only where our technical setup supports such collection and where applicable privacy requirements are satisfied.

C. Study Resources
Pustak X may provide educational materials, including notes, PYQs, documents, and links to external learning resources.
If you submit, upload, save, or interact with study materials through features we offer, we may process the information needed to provide those features.
Please avoid including personal or confidential information in materials you submit.

We may use the information described above for the following purposes:

  • To provide access to educational resources.
  • To manage user accounts, where available.
  • To organize and display study materials by subject, course, or semester.
  • To improve website navigation and user experience.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the platform.
  • To diagnose technical problems and fix errors.
  • To respond to questions, feedback, or support requests.
  • - To comply with applicable legal obligations.
We will not use personal information for purposes that are incompatible with the purposes explained in this policy unless we have an appropriate legal basis and provide any notices or obtain any consent required by law.
